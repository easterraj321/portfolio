    03 — About / Easter Raj

    SYSTEMS SERVING
    THE BUSINESS.

    Operational depth.
    Automation perspective.

    I work where frontline HR operations meet practical engineering—connecting HRIS platforms, eliminating manual spreadsheet bottlenecks, and keeping every process improvement tied to measurable time savings and audit accuracy.

    My operational foundation was built through sheer grit and self-reliance in Mumbai. While completing my Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) at the University of Mumbai, I worked early morning shifts and evening sales roles to fund my education, before spending five years managing accounts, statutory registers, and administration across large housing societies including Satellite Garden CHS, Zipgrid (Oberoi Splendor), and Aadya Habitat LLP (Alta Monte Tower C).

    Determined to build a long-term career in the corporate sector, I transitioned into ICICI Bank in July 2022. Within two months, I was selected among 33 branch officers to move into HR Operations & Recruitment. After a rigorous two-year evaluation, I was 1 of only 5 professionals retained from that cohort—managing 100+ monthly retail hires across the West Zone with 100% SLA adherence and zero audit discrepancies.

    At Kotak Neo (Kotak Securities Ltd.), after managing PAN-India pre-offer operations, I spotted how much time smart HR teams lost to repetitive manual Excel tasks. By pairing MS Copilot with Excel VBA, I built and deployed a production macro in 15 days that cut a 24-hour monthly process down to 1 hour—earning a dedicated mandate from senior leadership to automate workflows across HR Operations.
